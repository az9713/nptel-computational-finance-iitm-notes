From discounted cash flows to constrained portfolios, option-pricing algorithms, and Monte Carlo uncertainty.

NPTEL · Sourish Das and Bikramaditya Datta · Chennai Mathematical Institute and IIT Kanpur · IIT Madras coordination.

Learning route and prerequisites

Begin with calculus, linear algebra, probability, and basic programming. Chapters 1–6 develop financial objects and numerical linear algebra; 7–12 develop options, replication, and calibration; 13–18 develop calculus, stochastic pricing, and grids; 19–24 develop constrained optimization, arbitrage, and simulation. Paired R/Python labs and worked-problem recordings are mapped explicitly.

These are independent educational notes. Synthetic examples are identified; source statements, researched extensions, and original teaching constructions remain distinct. Read the exercises with their expandable solutions, reproduce the code, and consult the source map for recording provenance.

  9. 09. Path dependence and option laboratories

    accepted

    Explain exotic payoffs and implement valuation functions; distinguish path/state from terminal-price-only claims.

  10. 10. Financial equations as inverse problems

  12. 12. Constructing curves and volatility surfaces

  15. 15. Numerical integration for financial expectations
